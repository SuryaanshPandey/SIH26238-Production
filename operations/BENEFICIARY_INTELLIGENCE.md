# BENEFICIARY PROACTIVE INTELLIGENCE
## Targeted Outreach & Entitlement Discovery

---

## 1. Engine Purpose
Proactively identifies eligible tribal students enrolled in academic institutions (via AISHE, UDISE+, State Category DBs) who have **not yet claimed** their entitled scholarship benefits.

---

## 2. Pipeline
```
Institutional Enrolment Records (AISHE / UDISE+)
  │
  ▼
State Category & Domicile Database Matching
  │
  ▼
Scheme Rule Evaluation (Category + Income + Level)
  │
  ▼
Active Benefit / Claim Verification (Deduplication)
  │
  ▼
Candidate Scoring & Evidence Assembly
  │
  ▼
Desk Officer Review (Outreach Confirmation)
```

---

## 3. Critical Safety Rule
A detected candidate is **NEVER** automatically marked as entitled or disbursed funds without human outreach confirmation, formal application submission, and state verification.
